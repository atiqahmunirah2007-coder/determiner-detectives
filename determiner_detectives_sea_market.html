🎧 Mission 1: The Mystery of the Missing Labels

Find a quiet listening spot. Turn up your device volume, then press play. Listen carefully because every detail is a clue!

One sunny day under the sea, Benny Bot was preparing for a school picnic. He placed apples, bananas, oranges, and pencils on the market shelves. Suddenly, a magical sea breeze blew all the labels away! Benny Bot looked worried. “Oh no! How can I prepare the picnic?” he cried. A friendly dolphin smiled and said, “Don't worry, Benny! The Determiner Detectives can help you!”

Tip: Choose a warm English voice if available. Set your phone, tablet, or computer volume higher for louder playback.
